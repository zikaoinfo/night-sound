Sonora Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

Sonora records and analyses sound on your phone. Nothing leaves your phone. We don't collect, upload, sell or share any data, and the app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Microphone

Sonora uses the microphone only while you have started a session, to detect snoring and other sounds. Analysis happens on the device.

What is stored, and where

  • For each night: the detected sounds (time, loudness, type), a per-second loudness curve, and short audio clips of notable sounds.
  • Full-night audio only if you turn on "Keep the whole night's audio".
  • All of it lives in the app's private storage on your phone. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or Google) the same way as other app data, under your control.

Deleting your data

Delete any night from the History tab. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Health

Sonora is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnoea or any other condition. If you're worried about your sleep or breathing, talk to a doctor.

Children

Sonora collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/zikaoinfo/night-sound/issues.
